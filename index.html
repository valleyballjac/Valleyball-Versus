Pad: LT sprint, RT slide, A jump, B volley, Y spike, X dive, RB/LB camera.
Spike higher = steeper. On the ground it's nearly flat; at the top of a jump it drives down.
Dive costs you. It fires where the stick is pushing, not where you're facing — then no steering, no swinging, a skid you ride out, and you end up on the floor.
Don't stall a slide. Lose your momentum while holding it and you go down. Release it, or Space out, while you still have speed.
Show procedural dummy in Debug view puts the physics primitives back on screen — that is the real rig; the model is a skin over it.
Watch the bar. Sprinting drains it, climbing taxes it, jumping, diving and swinging cost a chunk. Walking is free. It refills slowly while you move and properly only when you stand still — and an empty bar means the button does nothing.
