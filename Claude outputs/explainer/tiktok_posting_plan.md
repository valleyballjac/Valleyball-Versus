## Upload settings

- **Upload from desktop** (TikTok Studio in a browser) rather than the phone app. It keeps more quality.
  - If you post from the phone, turn on the high-quality upload setting first.
  - TikTok Studio also lets you schedule posts ahead, so you can queue the whole week in one sitting.
- **Don't add TikTok text stickers or captions over the video.** The headline sits under TikTok's top tabs, and the card sits above the caption strip and clear of the like/comment/share column. Text added in the app will collide with them.
- **Cover.** Pick a frame from the first two seconds, where the opener headline is showing. That way the grid on your profile reads like a numbered series.
- **Sound.**
  - **Default:** post the music version. It becomes an "original sound" others can reuse, which is free promotion for the track and the game.
  - **Trending sound:** to ride one, upload the `_nomusic` file and add the sound in the editor.
- **AI-content toggle.**
  - The footage is real gameplay capture, so it doesn't need it.
  - The soundtrack is AI-generated (Suno). TikTok's rule targets realistic AI content, so labeling the music is your call; ticking it does no harm.
  - Check that your Suno plan covered commercial use if you'll earn from these.

## After posting

- **Profile.** Pin Part 1, and if your account has Playlists, group all eight as "How to Play".
- **Comments.** Answer questions with video replies, using a clip of the part that answers them.
- **Duets and Stitches.** Turn both on.
- **Watch Part 1's retention after about 48 hours.** If people swipe off in the first two seconds, try the bonus "The Payoff" as the series opener instead.
